Our Discipline

We don't do bolt-on parts. Every build is meticulously planned, engineered from raw steel, and dialed in on the dyno. Here's how we transform scrap into a mechanical masterpiece.

01

The Vision

We sit down with you, tear apart your ideas, and rebuild them into a cohesive concept. We define the donor geometry, stance, and purpose.

02

Tear Down & Fabrication

The donor bike is stripped to the frame. We cut, weld, and grind new subframes, custom tanks, and exhaust headers by hand.

03

The Heart

Engine rebuild, carb tuning, and complete rewiring with Motogadget systems for absolute reliability hidden within a minimalist aesthetic.

04

Paint & Delivery

Powder-coated frame, hand-finished metals, and rich leather upholstery. Finally, a brutal shakedown test before handing you the keys.
